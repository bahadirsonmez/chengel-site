Crossword · Vocabulary · A1–C1

Complete your garden by learning new words.

Chengel is a Swedish-style crossword for language learners. The clue comes in your language, the answer in the language you are learning. Every word you learn blooms in your garden.

For iPhone and iPad · iOS 17 or later · English, Turkish, German, Spanish

Clue in, answer out

Read the clue in your own language and fill in the answer in the language you learn. Pick any pair of English, Turkish, German or Spanish.

Five levels

Start at A1 and climb to C1. Every puzzle is built fresh from your level, so you never run out.

Words that come back

Words you miss return until they stick. Review them any time with flip cards.

A garden to grow

Keep a daily streak, earn blooms for the words you learn and plant them level by level. Your progress syncs through iCloud.

No subscription. Chengel is free to play: you can unlock extra letters with coins you earn, watch an optional ad for more, or buy coins and remove full-screen ads once.
