Questions

Which languages can I learn?

English, Turkish, German and Spanish, in any direction: the clues come in the language you speak, the answers in the one you learn. The app itself is available in the same four languages.

What levels are there?

Five, from A1 (beginner) to C1 (advanced). Every puzzle is built fresh from your level, so you never run out.

Is Chengel free?

Yes. You earn coins by playing and can spend them to open a letter or a word. Optional purchases add coins or remove full-screen ads; there is no subscription.

Do I need an account?

No. Your progress stays on your device and, if you use iCloud, syncs through your own iCloud storage. See the privacy policy for details.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone and iPad with iOS 17 or later. Get Chengel on the App Store.
